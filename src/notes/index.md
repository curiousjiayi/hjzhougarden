# What is a digital garden?

Over time, our internet spaces have become standardized, commercial, sanitized, and algorithm-driven. A digital garden returns us to the magic of the world wide web. It's a personal plot of land where we tend our own thoughts and string our own connections. Visitors are welcome to wander through the garden and to follow their curiosity.

Some pages in the garden are fully fleshed and edited essay. Some are baby carrots, and that's okay! Digital gardens are ever evolving and a space for us to share notes and invite discussion while we're still thinking. If you see something interesting, I'd love to hear from you. Also, there are easter eggs.
