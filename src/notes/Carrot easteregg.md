# Carrot

A carrot. I'm neutral about carrots. I can't get their texture right. When I roast them they get soggy. When I separate them in the oven, they get too crispy. In fried rice, they're my least favourite element. When paired with hummus raw, they lose to every other veggie. I do like them in soups (Chinese chicken soup made with a whole chicken and chunks of carrots and corn is the bee's knees).
But a garden feels incomplete without this humble root vegetable. So here, have a carrot.
