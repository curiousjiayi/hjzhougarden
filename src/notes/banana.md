# Banana

Banana is one of my two parrots. The other is [[Snowy]].

## Tidbits
- **Gotcha day:** mid-June, 2021
- **Favourite toy:** For a while it was a miniature blue Converse shoe. Now he likes anything that's shiny so he can regurgitate on it.
- **Favourite veggie:** N/A
- **Known tricks:**
 	- Step-up
 	- Spin
  - Tunnel (we recently got this down!)
  - Touch (chopstick, target training)
- **Known sayings:** Banana doesn't talk (in English).
- **Likes:** Feet. Fingernails, toenails. Shiny objects he can see his reflection in. 
- **Dislikes:** Direct eye contact.
