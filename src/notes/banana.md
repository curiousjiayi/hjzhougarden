# Banana

Banana is one of my two parrots. When he came to us, Banana was wing clipped and frightened. He ran away whenever we got too close, and would tremble in the face of unfamiliar situations. A few months after he settled into our home, he started to fly after [[Snowy]]. Snowy, being half a year older and fully flighted, could carve around our bedroom doors and family room pillars with ease. Banana constantly crashed into walls trying to follow him and would break the few flight feathers he managed to grow out. It broke my heart and is the reason why I believe you should never clip your bird's wings.

Over time Banana grew into himself. He has become a great flier, even better than Snowy (often giving David and I heart attacks when he darts into the bathroom as the door is closing); he found his favourite objects (feet, anything reflective); and while he's still a bit of a scaredy-cat, he now stands his ground when I prod him to pick a different sleeping branch at night.
