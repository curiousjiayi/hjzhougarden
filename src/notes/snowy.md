# Snowy

Snowy is one of my two small parrots; the other is [[Banana|banana]].

## Tidbits
- **Gotcha day:** December 21st, 2020 (Covid baby)
- **Favourite toy:** Wooden blocks with small round bell at the end
- **Favourite veggie:** Cilantro (first vegetable he ate, after we stuffed it into the holes of the wooden blocks of his favourite toy)
- **Known tricks:**
 	- Step-up
 	- Spin
  - Tunnel
  - Upside down
  - Fetch (paperclip)
  - Touch (chopstick, target training)
- **Known sayings:**
	- Step up
 	- Watcha doing
 	- Baby bird
 	- Snowy
 	- Banana (regressed)
 	- Various demonic mumblings
- **Likes:** Being sung to, hanging out on David's shoulder, millet, cooked egg, running water
- **Dislikes:** Being touched, Banana being mean
