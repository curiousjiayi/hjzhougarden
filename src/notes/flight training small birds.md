
# Flight training small birds

Flight training small birds is straightforward but requires patience. There are only three principles you need to remember during training:
1. Birds are smart and capable.
2. You are big and scary, and hence need to project trust.
3. Learning what this big scary hairless animal wants you to do via interspecies charades is hard, so have some empathy.

These are the steps that I used to train my two budgies for flight and recall.

---

## I. Master the Step-up

1. With your bird in your cage, offer them some tasty snack, like millet (for budgies).
2. Do this a few times over a few days.
3. Then, offer your index finger with your knuckle facing outwards towards your bird. Put your finger slowly up to your bird near their belly. Offer them millet held in your other hand, holding it so they can access the millet by leaning slightly over your finger.
4. Move the millet a bit farther away so they have to lean farther.
5. Move the millet farther away so they have to put a leg on your finger to reach it.
6. Move the millet farther away so they have to step on your finger fully.
7. Give them enthusiastic praise, like "good bird!"
